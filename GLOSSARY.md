# Portfolio

A professional portfolio site that presents Leke's solo and team work to engineers, hiring managers, and academic reviewers.

## Language

**Project**:
A piece of work Leke built, solo or with a team, that is presented on the portfolio as a card on the index.
_Avoid_: Work, item, entry

**Case Study**:
The full page for a Project that explains the problem, Leke's role, key decisions, outcome, and links.
_Avoid_: Detail page, write-up

**Admin**:
The private, owner-only area where Leke creates and edits Projects and Case Studies, reorders Projects, publishes or drafts them, and uploads images and the CV.
_Avoid_: Dashboard, CMS, backend

**Order**:
The manual sequence of Projects set by Leke in the Admin, used for the index and for previous/next navigation on Case Studies.
_Avoid_: Rank, sort, position

**Solo / Team**:
The label on every Project saying whether Leke built it alone or with others.
_Avoid_: Individual, group, collaborative

**My Role**:
The section of a Team Project's Case Study listing what Leke personally built.
_Avoid_: Contribution, responsibilities

**Summary**:
The opening of a Case Study giving outcome, stack, and role for a quick skim.
_Avoid_: Overview, TL;DR

**Reasoning**:
The Case Study section explaining the decisions made and why, aimed at academic reviewers.
_Avoid_: Rationale, decisions

**Technical Depth**:
The final Case Study section covering implementation detail for engineers, with prominent code links.
_Avoid_: Implementation, deep dive
